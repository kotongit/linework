How to install and open Linework

1. Drag Linework onto the Applications folder.
2. Open Linework from Applications. macOS will say it can't check the app and won't open it. Click Done.
3. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security.
4. Scroll down to Security and click Open Anyway next to Linework. Confirm with your password or Touch ID.

You only need to do this the first time. Linework isn't signed with a paid Apple certificate, which is why macOS asks.

After an update, macOS may ask again for access to the folder holding your library. Say yes, and everything will be where you left it.

Linework needs macOS 27 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
